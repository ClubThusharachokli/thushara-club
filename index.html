About our club

Thushara means a drop of dew. We began small and grow with every programme: performances, sports meets and festivals that bring the neighbourhood together.

ArtsMusic, dance, drama and festival programmes.
SportsTournaments, practice sessions and fitness meets.
CommunityOpen to everyone in and around Chokli.
YouthA place for young people to learn and lead.
